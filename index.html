      About TRX on Tron

      Every Tron transaction uses bandwidth (its size in bytes) and contract calls use energy. Each account gets 600 bandwidth free a day; energy comes only from staking TRX (or renting it). What isn't covered is paid by burning TRX: at today's prices, 0.001 TRX a byte and 0.0001 TRX per unit of energy.

      A USDT transfer uses 65,000–130,000 energy, so 6.5–13 TRX with none staked. A Legacy Mesh send uses about 226,000 energy (22.6 TRX) plus its LayerZero fee of 5–6 TRX, which must be TRX; the approval before it uses about 100,000 (10 TRX). The page shows the exact amount for each transfer before you sign.

      To get TRX: buy it on an exchange and withdraw it to your Tron address. Energy rental services sell energy for less than burning TRX costs, but you still need TRX for the LayerZero fee.
